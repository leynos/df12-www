Carbon Migration

Font Awesome to Carbon icon replacements

This page documents every unique Font Awesome icon that was used across the Netsuke prototype and the Carbon Design System replacement now applied to the site through Iconify. Where Carbon does not offer a true 1:1 brand or metaphor, the replacement is intentionally creative and called out as such.

Method

Firecrawl confirmed the official Carbon icon library and the Iconify Carbon collection pages. The final carbon:* names on this page were also checked against the published @iconify-json/carbon package so the identifiers are valid.

  • 68 unique Font Awesome icons mapped
  • 29 exact matches
  • 13 exact-ish semantic matches
  • 26 creative substitutions
Sources: Carbon icon library and Iconify Carbon collection.
